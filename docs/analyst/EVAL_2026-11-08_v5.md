## Reading

- The narrowing was built to answer A1-A5 near-misses. Three adversarial safety reviews then
  tightened A1 and A3 until each review's attacks were refused. The Yunlin-only scope
  exemption and the spillover exemption were dropped, and A3 became an allow-list. On v5, every
  A1 and A3 near-miss is refused at the guards, as in round 9: the fresh set gives no evidence
  that A1/A3 gained anything. A2, A4 and A5 near-misses are answered (27 of 30 rows).
- Deployed pipeline across independent sets: v3 76.7 % with 4 unsafe per run; v4 85.6 %,
  0 unsafe; v5 86.7 %, 0 unsafe. The sets differ, so this is a range, not a trend.
- The remaining gap to 97 % is 8 guard over-refusals (A1, A3, one A4) and 4 LLM routing
  errors.
